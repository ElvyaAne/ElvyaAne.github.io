hobby · swimming

Swimming

I've been swimming since I was about six. It's very physically demanding, which is what I like about it.

It's delayed gratification to the max: it takes a while to see results, but once you do, they're hard to ignore, and I just love that.

Watch the laps add up. The results bar takes its time, just like the real thing.

hobby · gym

Gym

In university I needed another way to exercise, so I opted for the gym. Lifting is fun, and it's nice to set little goals for yourself and feel proud once you accomplish them.

Set a little goal, then load the bar until you hit it.

hobby · content creation

Content creation

I've been on and off with content creation my whole life, but this past year I've been more consistent with it.

I like being able to talk about a topic, post about it, and then discuss it with strangers. It's given me insightful ideas I don't think I could get anywhere else.

Post a topic and see who shows up to talk about it.
